🎮 HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LIFE PROGRESSION:
  Age 0:     Congenital check (0-1% affected)
  Age 15+:   Chronic disease checks (0.01-0.1% annually)
  Age 30+:   Acute crisis checks (0.01-0.3% annually)
  Every Yr:  Disease penalties applied (driftChronicDiseases)
  On Event:  Relationship impacts, family stress, caregiver roles
  Lifetime:  Medical costs drain income, disability affects employment

OUTCOME DISTRIBUTION:
  Full recovery (50-60% of survivors)
    → Resume normal life, monitoring for recurrence
  
  Partial recovery (20-30%)
    → Reduced employment capacity (-50%)
    → Ongoing medical costs
    → Possible caregiver needs
  
  Permanent disability (5-15%)
    → Unemployable, dependency on family
    → Ongoing caregiver burden
    → Mental health stress
  
  Death (5-70% by region)
    → Nordic: 10% (best treatment)
    → Fragile: 70% (poor access)
