# System Analysis: Root Cause of High Suicide Rates

## Key Finding
The suicide rate of 600-700/100K is NOT primarily a suicide calculation problem. It's a **symptom of a broken base system**.

## Evidence
- Baseline suicide probability: 12.6/100K (correct)
- Empirical suicide rate: 612/100K (48x too high)
- **BUT**: 81.8% of people die before age 30
- Suicide is only 0.6% of deaths ages 0-30

## Root Cause: Population Baseline is Catastrophic
- Birth cards specify Nordic with 83 year life expectancy
- Actual: 82% mortality before age 30
- This is equivalent to **Sub-Saharan Africa**, not Nordic

## Why This Matters
When 82% of the population dies in early years, the **survivors are naturally the most vulnerable** - those who accumulated suicidal crises and survived attempts become part of the surviving population statistics.

So high suicide rates are actually a *selection effect* from catastrophic early mortality, not intrinsic to the suicide system itself.

## What Needs Fixing (In Order)
1. **Base mortality rates** - Too high across all causes
2. **Health initialization and drift** - Something is draining health
3. **Employment/economic system** - Unemployment too high (only 13.7% employed at age 30)
4. **Mental health baseline** - Drops from 88 → 64 too quickly with age

## Next Steps
1. Accept that this is a **data validation issue**, not a balanced game yet
2. Fix base mortality first (should aim for 90%+ survival to age 30 in Nordic)
3. Then re-test all systems (suicide, addiction, accidents, etc.)
4. Finally calibrate and balance

## Systems Status
- ✅ Suicide calculation: Correct
- ✅ Community/relationship weighting: Correct
- ⚠️ Substance abuse system: Created but disabled (has bugs)
- ⚠️ Accidents system: Created but disabled (too lethal)
- ❌ BASE MORTALITY: Catastrophically wrong
- ❌ EMPLOYMENT SYSTEM: Only 13.7% rate to age 30
- ❌ HEALTH BASELINES: Dropping too fast with age

## Recommendation
Don't balance individual systems against incorrect base rates. Fix the foundation first.
